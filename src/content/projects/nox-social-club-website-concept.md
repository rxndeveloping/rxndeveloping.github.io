Nox is a complete nightlife concept for a cocktail bar and listening room. Its four pages cover the venue story, signature cocktail catalogue, weekly event programme, and a four-step reservation journey for different rooms and service formats. Every page supports shareable English, Italian, French, and Spanish URLs.
