Lume is an advanced four-page restaurant demonstration with an immersive home page, seasonal menus, a dedicated dining-experience page, and a four-step reservation flow. Guests can choose an experience, date, party size, and time before completing their details and receiving a simulated confirmation code. Every page supports shareable English, Italian, French, and Spanish URLs. It is a fictional portfolio concept rather than commissioned client work.
