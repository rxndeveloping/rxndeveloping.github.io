Braci & Basilico is a high-energy demonstration website created to show a direction that is intentionally different from a conventional restaurant website. It includes interactive menu filtering, dough and event storytelling, responsive content, and a simulated reservation experience. It is a fictional portfolio concept rather than commissioned client work.
