Braci & Basilico is a bold four-page pizzeria demonstration with a high-impact home page, filterable menu, weekly event programme, and a four-step booking flow. Guests can choose their format, date, party size, and time before receiving a dynamic summary and simulated confirmation. Every page supports shareable English, Italian, French, and Spanish URLs. It is a fictional portfolio concept rather than commissioned client work.
